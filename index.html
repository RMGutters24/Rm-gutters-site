Community Outreach

Helping Abilene grow stronger — one person, one home, and one opportunity at a time.

Our Abilene Mission

Our long-term vision is to help people in Abilene get off the streets and get back on their feet. Some people just need a chance — and we’re building a place where they can find that chance.

Second Chance Employment

We aim to open doors for those who are struggling by offering real work opportunities. Gutter cleaning, light labor, small repairs — we teach, train, and help people rebuild confidence through steady income.

Future Work Program

Over the next few months, we plan to build a structured program for people who want to work but need support. This includes rides to job sites, tools, training, and mentorship.

Community Support

We connect with local churches, veteran groups, and shelters to identify people who genuinely want a fresh start. Every job we take on helps us expand these outreach efforts.

How You Can Help

You can support our outreach by booking services, sharing our business, or donating through Venmo. 100% of donated funds go toward helping someone in need in Abilene.
