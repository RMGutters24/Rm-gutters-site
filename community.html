Veterans & Outreach

RM Gutters is proud to be veteran-owned and focused on giving people a second chance. This business was created to open doors for folks who are often overlooked because of their past.

Why We Exist

We know what it’s like when employers won’t even look past your background check. RM Gutters is here to prove that hard work, honesty, and accountability matter more than a mistake on paper.

Veteran Values on Every Job

  • Show up on time
  • Respect for your home and property
  • Attention to detail and safety
  • Clear communication, no surprises

Second Chance Mission

As the business grows, the goal is to provide work opportunities for local people who need a shot at rebuilding their lives. If someone is willing to work, learn, and show up, we want to be part of that second chance.

Want to Support the Mission?

Every gutter cleaned, every light install, and every junk haul helps support a small local business with a bigger purpose.
