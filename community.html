Veterans & Community Outreach

This part of RM Gutters is all about taking care of the people who took care of us. We want Veterans — and anyone needing a second chance — to feel seen, welcomed, and supported the moment they land here.

Our Mission

Life after service isn’t always simple. RM Gutters gives back by helping local Veterans and community members facing hardship, emergencies, or transition.

How Donations Help

100% of donated funds stay local — helping with food, fuel, emergency bills, hotel nights in crisis, and real-life needs.

Transparency

RM Gutters is a veteran-owned small business, not a nonprofit. Donations are voluntary and not tax-deductible — but every dollar helps someone in Abilene.

💙 Donate via Venmo @rmgutters

Second Chances Start With Real Work

Our long-term goal is to create opportunities for people to rebuild their lives through honest work, accountability, and support — one job at a time.
