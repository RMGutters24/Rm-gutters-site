Supporting Veterans in Need

Helping our brothers & sisters who served — right here in Abilene.

Our Mission

Life after service isn’t always simple. RM Gutters gives back by helping local Veterans who are struggling with basic needs, emergencies, or finding support.

How Donations Help

100% of donated funds go directly toward helping Veterans in Abilene — including food, gas, bills, and emergency needs.

Transparency

RM Gutters is a veteran-owned small business (not a nonprofit). Donations are voluntary and not tax-deductible — but every dollar helps someone local.

💙 Donate via Venmo @rmgutters

Community Outreach

Building a stronger Abilene — one job, one person, one second chance at a time.

Our Abilene Mission

We believe everyone deserves a shot at starting over. RM Gutters is working toward offering real opportunities for people trying to get off the streets.

Second Chance Employment

Our plan includes hiring individuals who need a fresh start — giving them work, training, stability, and support.

Training Program

As the business grows, a formal work program will provide skills training, rides to job sites, tools, and mentorship.

Community Resources

We work with churches, shelters, and outreach groups to connect people to support, meals, and emergency help.

How You Can Help

Booking our services directly fuels this outreach. Donations support supplies, food, basic needs, and future employment opportunities.
