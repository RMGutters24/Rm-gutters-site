Veterans & Community Outreach

Helping our brothers and sisters who served — and building second chances for people right here in Abilene.

Our Mission

Life after service isn’t always simple. RM Gutters gives back by helping local Veterans and community members who are struggling with basic needs, emergencies, or finding support. We believe nobody should feel like they’re starting over alone.

How Donations Help

100% of donated funds go directly toward helping people in our local area — including fuel, food, emergency bills, hotel nights in a crisis, and other real-life needs. Every dollar stays right here in our community.

Transparency

RM Gutters is a veteran-owned small business (not a nonprofit). Donations are voluntary and not tax-deductible — but they allow us to step in quickly when someone in Abilene needs help. We’re committed to being honest about where support goes and who it helps.

Community Outreach

Building a stronger Abilene — one job, one person, one second chance at a time.

Our Abilene Mission

We believe everyone deserves a shot at starting over. RM Gutters is working toward offering real work opportunities and second chances — especially for people who feel written off because of their past. Hard work, honesty, and accountability can change a life. We want to be part of that change here in Abilene.

Second Chances Start With Real Work

Our long-term goal is to grow RM Gutters into a place where we can bring people in, train them, and help them get off the streets and back into steady work — one ladder, one roofline, one job at a time. When you hire RM Gutters or support our outreach, you’re helping build that future.
